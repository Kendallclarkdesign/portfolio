Max Noir, a creator site built for brand deals.

A UGC creator and music curator needed one link that closes brand deals. I designed it with the client across four concepts, then built and shipped it.

Client
Max Noir
Role
Design, build, content
Stack
HTML, CSS, JS, Netlify
Live site
iammaxnoir.com

The problem

Max applies for UGC gigs and pitches brands, but the proof was scattered across Instagram, TikTok, and a link-in-bio page. A brand manager had to dig to find relevant work. The site needed to do one job: show the right work in seconds and make it easy to say yes.

Decisions

  • Four concepts, one pick. I mocked up four whole-site directions and let the client choose. The pick was "Pop Shop": heavy type, hard-offset shadows, and colors pulled from the hero photo.
  • A dedicated UGC page. It is the link sent with gig applications, so it opens on proof: phone-frame clips and a brands carousel with real logos, not a generic bio.
  • Real content only. 42 portfolio tiles came from the @iammaxnoir Instagram, with the client's consent. Brands with no posted work stay labeled Sample instead of faking proof.
  • Spec first. A MASTER.md (content and behavior) and DESIGN.md (visual) are the source of truth. Any change updates the spec, then the code.
  • Editable without a rebuild. Links, portfolio items, releases, and filters all live in one config object, so content updates are a one-file edit.

Craft details

  • Every button and chip carries an icon, drawn from one inline SVG sprite for consistency and zero extra requests.
  • Pointer-tilt and extruded headline effects are switched off for reduced-motion users.
  • Light and dark themes built on shared color tokens, with the palette checked for contrast.
  • Social feeds: an official TikTok embed, plus a scheduled function that refreshes Instagram posts every six hours.

Outcome

The site is live and in use as the link Max sends with UGC applications. I have not yet captured conversion or reply-rate data, so I am not claiming numbers here. Next up: a shorter contact form (it currently asks for eight required fields) and a custom domain.
