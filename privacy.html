Privacy and Terms

Short and plain.

Last updated October 2026

What this site collects

Nothing automatically. There are no cookies, no analytics, and no trackers. The contact form opens your own email app with a message addressed to me, so I only receive what you choose to send.

The site does not store anything in your browser either.

Third parties

Fonts load from Google Fonts, and the 3D animation on the home page loads a library from jsDelivr, which means those services can see your IP address when a page loads. Links to LinkedIn and client sites are governed by those sites' own policies.

Using the content

The designs, writing, and code on this site are mine unless noted. Client work is shown with permission or with placeholder data in place of real customer information. Please ask before reusing anything.

Case study results describe my own work on those projects. The site is provided as is, with no warranty.
