About Kendall

New York designer.
Creative thinker.
Technical builder.

I'm Kendall Clark, a Senior UX/UI Designer, AI Product Designer, and Front-End Developer based in the Bronx, New York.

I came up through the creative scene in Bushwick, Brooklyn, fashion, art, music, performance. That background isn't a footnote to my design career. It's the foundation of it. I bring a perspective to product design that most designers trained purely in tech simply don't have: an understanding of culture, identity, aesthetics, and what it means to build something that actually resonates with people.

Professionally, I've spent 7+ years designing digital products for some of the biggest names in fintech, American Express and Citi, and building independent brands through my creative studio 21 Connect Digital. I've led UX on fraud investigation tools that cut analyst workload in half. I've redesigned banking flows that measurably lifted customer satisfaction. I've shipped mobile apps, e-commerce stores, and everything in between.

I hold a UX Design Immersive Certificate from General Assembly (2022) and Figma Professional and Front-End Development certifications from LinkedIn Learning. I'm fluent in Angular, TypeScript, SCSS, and React, which means I don't just hand off designs. I build them.

Currently exploring AI-integrated product design, conversational UI, LLM interface patterns, AI-augmented workflows. This is where UX is heading, and I'm already there.

Outside of work: I run Haus of Alt, a handmade alternative jewelry brand and creative community in NYC. I make music under the name kyndall. I care about spirituality, self-expression, and building things that mean something.

Available for full-time roles, W2 contracts, and freelance projects. Remote, hybrid, or on-site in New York.
