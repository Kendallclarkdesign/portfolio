About Kendall

Designer on both coasts. Technical builder.

I'm Kendall Clark, a Senior UX/UI Designer and front-end developer from Georgia, and I split my time between Los Angeles and New York.

For the last 12 years I've moved between Los Angeles and New York, working in fashion, art, music, and performance. That background isn't a footnote to my design career, it's the foundation of it. I bring a perspective to product design that most designers trained purely in tech don't have: an understanding of culture, identity, and what it means to build something that resonates with people.

Professionally, I've spent 8+ years designing digital products, including enterprise fintech work at American Express and Citi, and independent brands through my creative studio, Towpath Creative. At American Express, I worked on a fraud investigation platform redesign that cut investigator review time by half. At Citi, I worked on banking flows that lifted customer satisfaction scores by 15%.

I hold a UX Design Immersive Certificate from General Assembly (2022) and Figma Professional and Front-End Development certifications from LinkedIn Learning. I'm fluent in Angular, TypeScript, SCSS, and React, which means I don't just hand off designs, I can build them.

Outside of work: I design for Haus of Alt, a handmade alternative jewelry brand and creative community in NYC.

Available for full-time roles, W2 contracts, and freelance projects. Remote, hybrid, or on-site in Los Angeles or New York.
