About Kendall

New York designer. Technical builder.

I'm Kendall Clark, a Senior UX/UI Designer and front-end developer based in the Bronx, New York.

I came up through the creative scene in Bushwick, Brooklyn, fashion, art, music, performance. That background isn't a footnote to my design career, it's the foundation of it. I bring a perspective to product design that most designers trained purely in tech don't have: an understanding of culture, identity, and what it means to build something that resonates with people.

Professionally, I've spent 7+ years designing digital products, including enterprise fintech work at American Express and Citi, and independent brands through my creative studio, 21 Connect Digital. At American Express, I worked on a fraud investigation platform redesign that cut investigator review time by half. At Citi, I worked on banking flows that lifted customer satisfaction scores by 15%.

I hold a UX Design Immersive Certificate from General Assembly (2022) and Figma Professional and Front-End Development certifications from LinkedIn Learning. I'm fluent in Angular, TypeScript, SCSS, and React, which means I don't just hand off designs, I can build them.

Outside of work: I run Haus of Alt, a handmade alternative jewelry brand and creative community in NYC. I make music under the name kyndall. I care about spirituality, self-expression, and building things that mean something.

Available for full-time roles, W2 contracts, and freelance projects. Remote, hybrid, or on-site in New York.
