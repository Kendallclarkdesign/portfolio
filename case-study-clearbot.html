ClearBot, a Shopify storefront for one hero product.

A single-product pool skimmer store, designed so the product, the price of risk, and the next step are clear in one screen.

Client
ClearBot
Product
SolarSkim Pro
Role
Storefront design, content
Platform
Shopify

The problem

A robotic pool skimmer is a considered purchase from an unfamiliar brand. Shoppers need to see what it does, trust the seller, and know what happens if it does not work out, all before they scroll.

Decisions

  • One promise in the hero. "Your pool cleans itself. Finally." sits over a real pool scene, with one button to the product.
  • Risk answered up front. A trust strip right under the hero covers free shipping, 30-day returns with no restocking fee, human support, and one-day dispatch.
  • A short menu. Home, The Product, Contact, and the two policy pages. For a one-product store, anything more is noise.
  • Announcement bar. The shipping and returns offer repeats at the very top, so it is seen on every page.

Beyond the store

I also built short TikTok video compositions for ClearBot in code with Remotion, so the same product story could run as paid and organic video.

Outcome

The storefront is built and styled to this brief. I do not have sales or conversion figures to share, so none are claimed here.
