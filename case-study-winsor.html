Winsor Pilates, a membership site built around one free trial.

An at-home Pilates streaming service needs a visitor to understand the method, trust it, and start a trial, all on one page.

Client
Winsor Pilates
Product
Streaming membership
Role
Web Designer
Platform
Uscreen

The problem

Winsor Pilates sells a monthly or annual streaming membership to people who want to work out at home. Most visitors arrive curious but unsure. They need to see what the method is, who teaches it, and what joining costs them, with as little risk as possible.

Page structure

The homepage follows the order a hesitant visitor asks questions in:

  • Promise first. "The Original Pilates-at-Home Method" with a plain claim: 20 minutes a day, 3 times a week.
  • One action. "Start 14-Day FREE Trial" repeats down the page, so there is never a question about what to do next.
  • Credibility. The instructor's story and the method, explained before anything is sold.
  • Proof before price. Member testimonials and transformation photos sit ahead of the plans.
  • Simple navigation. Home, Catalog, Community, About Us, Calendar, and Reviews, with Sign in and Join set apart on the right.

Working inside a platform

The site runs on Uscreen, a hosted video membership platform, so the sign-in, catalog, and checkout follow its patterns. The design work happens in the landing page and brand layer: type scale, hero imagery, blue and white color from the logo, and the order of the sections.

Outcome

The site is live. I do not have trial or subscription figures to share, so none are claimed here.
