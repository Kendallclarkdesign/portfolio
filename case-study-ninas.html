The Brief

No online presence. No way for customers to find her.

Nina runs a residential cleaning business in the Bronx with a loyal base of word-of-mouth clients. She had no website, no Google Business Profile, and no way for new customers in her area to discover her. The goal was to build a full digital presence from scratch — brand direction, a live website, Google optimization, and an SEO foundation strong enough to compete with established local cleaning services.

Discovery

Understanding the client and the market

I started with a discovery session to understand Nina's services, pricing structure, target neighborhoods, and what made her different. She offered residential cleaning, move-in/move-out cleans, and deep cleaning, with strong attention to detail and consistent reliability. Her clients described her work as thorough and trustworthy.

A competitive audit of Bronx-area cleaning services showed a crowded field of generic sites with stock photography, no defined brand, and weak local SEO. Most weren't claiming their Google Business Profiles or targeting neighborhood-level search terms. That was the gap.

Brand Direction

Sparkle Skyline — professional, not corporate

The brand direction I landed on was "Sparkle Skyline" — a name concept capturing the freshness of a cleaned space against the Bronx cityscape. The palette is periwinkle and navy, a combination that reads trustworthy and polished without feeling cold or institutional. Most competitors defaulted to teal or green. The periwinkle created immediate visual differentiation.

Typography pairs a clean geometric sans for headlines with a readable body font. The tone across all copy is warm, direct, and service-forward — no corporate filler, just clear information about what Nina offers and how to book.

Development

Built fast, built to last — pure HTML/CSS/JS

The site was built in clean HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript — no framework overhead, maximum speed. Pages built: Home, About, Services, Book Now, Contact, and a Thank You confirmation page. Each page was structured for both usability and search performance: semantic HTML, proper heading hierarchy, and meta tags on every page.

One of the more interesting technical problems came during deployment. A persistent JavaScript injection bug was causing the navigation to malfunction across page loads — the nav state was being reset incorrectly via a dynamic script. The fix was to hardcode the nav HTML directly into each page rather than injecting it via JS. It's a simpler solution than most developers would reach for, but it eliminated the bug entirely and actually improved page load performance.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages at no cost to the client, with a custom domain and GitHub's built-in CDN. It's fully mobile-responsive and achieves strong Lighthouse scores out of the box.

Local SEO

Built to be found in the Bronx

The SEO strategy focused on local search dominance — the specific neighborhoods and search terms that Bronx residents use when looking for cleaning services. This meant targeting terms like "cleaning service near me Bronx," "move-out cleaning Bronx NY," and "house cleaning Bedford Park" rather than generic national terms.

Google Business Profile was set up and fully optimized: business category, service areas, hours, photos, and a complete services list. A three-post-per-week GBP content plan was developed to maintain profile activity — the most active week her profile had ever had.

Citation building was executed across major local directories (Yelp, Bing Places, Apple Maps, Angi, Thumbtack, and others) to establish consistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone) data across the web. Consistent citations are one of the primary ranking signals for local pack results.

Outcome

From invisible to indexed in weeks

Nina went from zero digital presence to a live website, an active Google Business Profile, and consistent directory listings across the Bronx — all within a single project engagement. The site is live, fast, and built to grow. The SEO foundation is in place for long-term local visibility growth.
